About our program

A nurturing place to explore, learn, and grow independent

Wonderful Montessori Preschool provides a nurturing environment where children explore, learn, and develop independence through age-appropriate, Montessori-inspired activities.

Our days balance focused hands-on work with movement, art, music, and outdoor play — so children build early academic skills alongside the confidence, curiosity, and social ease that carry them into Kindergarten and beyond.

Hands-on learning

Children learn by doing, with concrete materials they choose and repeat freely.

Early academics

Phonics, early reading, writing, numbers, geography, and science at each child's pace.

Practical life skills

Pouring, buttoning, tidying, caring for the classroom — the work that builds independence.

Social & emotional growth

Sharing, turn-taking, and kind words practiced daily in a small mixed-age community.

Creativity & problem-solving

Open-ended art, music, and building invite children to try, adjust, and try again.

Kindergarten readiness

Focus, routines, and self-help skills, so the first day of Kindergarten feels familiar.
